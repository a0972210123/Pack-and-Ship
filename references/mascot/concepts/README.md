# Telo grayscale concept set — round 1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-07-26. These are
comparison concepts, not approved production art.

**Superseded direction note:** after reviewing this set, the owner lowered
Telo's age from 18–25 to 12–15 and requested a much simpler line-and-silhouette
design with fewer details. Keep these images only for comparing broad head,
body, pose and armour ideas. Do not carry their dense individual plates,
surface rendering or adult proportions into round 2.

All five prompts used the recovered Telo character brief plus three reference
images:

1. the first-party Tela Aurea Lab logo for softened-diamond geometry and the
   curled-logo transformation;
2. a curled Philippine pangolin for curl anatomy;
3. a pangolin museum specimen for scale overlap and region transitions.

The shared prompt locked the 4.75-head young-adult proportion, upright compact
monster morphology, fast movement, pangolin-first/feline-front face, round
pupils, four digits, tiny claws, bare hands and abdomen, regional armour map,
0.8× fully armoured tail, grayscale material hierarchy, no clothing, no
accessories and no body emblem.

Two-legged sprint interpretation. Use it to judge acceleration posture, tail as

The first generated attempt accidentally used a quadruped main pose and was

### `telo-v3-regal.png`

Broader chest and calmer young-king presence. Use it to judge the shoulder line,

human-muscular than the base brief, so treat that as an exploration rather than
an established feature.

### `telo-v4-pangolin.png`

Pangolin-forward head and shell continuity. Use it to judge the front-versus-side
face transition, restrained mane and continuous back-to-tail armour.

### `telo-v5-playful.png`

Open-hand, lively and approachable interpretation. Use it to judge Telo's
sharing personality, ear spread and friendly motion language.

## Review method

Do not choose one image wholesale. Review by feature:

- head and side profile;
- eye and ear treatment;
- torso and leg proportion;
- mane and shoulder plates;
- back-to-tail transition;
- curled pose and central negative space;
- overall age and personality.

The next round should combine the preferred features into one narrower
silhouette direction before refining anatomy or making a turnaround.
