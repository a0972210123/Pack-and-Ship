# Telo simplified concept set — round 2

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-07-26. These are
comparison concepts, not approved production art.

## Shared prompt

All five versions use the current Telo brief:

- age 12–15;
- provisional 4.75-head upright adolescent proportion;
- compact monster-like silhouette with short powerful legs and quick movement;
- pangolin-first side profile and softly feline front view;
- large round pupils, airplane ears and a gently happy neutral expression;
- four digits per hand and foot with tiny claw tips;
- bare hands, cheeks and abdomen;
- 0.8× fully armoured tail;
- regional armour map and softened diamond/shield language;
- natural, unclothed and without an emblem.

Round 2 specifically requires vector-friendly contour art, flat white/light-gray
fills, minimal interior lines, no texture and no rendered material detail.

The first-party Tela Aurea Lab logo was used for geometry and curl logic.
Round-1 V4 was used only for broad identity and front/side face relationships;
its adult age, dense scales and pencil rendering were explicitly excluded.

## Versions

### `telo-r2-v1-balanced.png`

Balanced adolescent baseline. It retains the most plate divisions in this set
and therefore acts as the upper limit for acceptable line density.

### `telo-r2-v2-friendly.png`

Softer head and body curves, relaxed hands and the strongest friendly/sharing
read. Fewer armour divisions than V1.

### `telo-r2-v3-agile.png`

More directional head, swept-back armour and a ready-to-move pose. Strongest
quick/agile silhouette while keeping short powerful legs.

### `telo-r2-v4-minimal.png`

The fewest armour divisions and cleanest small-icon silhouette. This is the
lower detail limit; its back armour is more implied than explicit.

### `telo-r2-v5-logo-geometric.png`

Broad softened-diamond panels and the clearest open-center curled ring. Strongest
visual connection to the Tela Aurea Lab logo without placing an emblem on the
body.

## Suggested review

Select features rather than a whole image:

- age and head proportion;
- front/side muzzle;
- body and leg silhouette;
- amount of mane;
- acceptable number of armour divisions;
- shoulder treatment;
- tail segmentation;
- curled central negative space.

The likely production range is between V2, V3 and V5. V1 and V4 define the
high- and low-detail boundaries.
